## Do I enter hours per day or for the whole request?

Enter **Total Hours** for the whole request. For three eight-hour days, enter **24**. Weekend and holiday exclusions change the suggested hours, not the report's start and end dates.

## Why do my dates overlap another request?

A pending or approved request already includes one of those dates. This applies even to different leave types or part-day requests. Open **History** and choose **All Types** to find it. Denied requests do not block those dates.

## Can I edit or cancel a submitted report?

Leaves has no edit, cancel, or withdraw action. [Create a support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves) with the leave type, dates, and correction needed.

## Why was my request approved immediately?

Your department may use automatic approval. Check **Approval type** under **Quick Actions** on your dashboard. Otherwise, your request stays **PendingApproval** until a reviewer approves or denies it.

## When will I receive an email?

Leaves emails you when a request is approved or denied, including automatic approvals. You can check **History** without waiting for an email. Contact your department's reviewer if you want to discuss a denial.

## Why did my balance not change after approval?

Balances come from UCPath. Submitting or approving a request does not recalculate them in Leaves.

## Why am I missing a tab or faculty member?

Your role and department or cluster assignments control what you can see. [Contact Leaves support](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves) and describe the access you expected.

## Why can I not submit a report?

Check the message in the form and complete the required fields. When hours are required, enter more than zero and no more than 240 hours per request. If the message says your faculty record or reporting department is missing, contact Leaves support.

For the main steps, see the [faculty guide](/documentation/leaves/faculty-guide) or [Chair and CAO guide](/documentation/leaves/chair-and-cao-guide).
