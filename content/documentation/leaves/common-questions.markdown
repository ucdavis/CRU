For faculty, department approvers, and CAOs. If your question is not covered here, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves).

## Do I enter hours per day or hours for the whole request?

Enter **Total Hours** for the whole request. For three 8-hour workdays, enter **24**.

For a date range, **Exclude weekends** and **Exclude university holidays** help calculate the suggested hours shown in the form. Review the suggestion and enter the total yourself. These options do not change the start and end dates of your report.

When hours are required, the total must be greater than zero and no more than 240 hours for one request. See [Submitting a leave request](/documentation/leaves/submitting-a-leave-request-v2).

## Why does Leaves say my dates overlap another request?

Leaves checks your dates against existing **PendingApproval** and **Approved** requests. Any shared date counts as an overlap, including the first and last day of a range. The check applies across leave types and also applies to requests for part of a day.

[![Leave report form displaying an error because the selected dates overlap an existing request.](/leaves/guides/overlapping-dates.png "Check existing reports before submitting overlapping dates")](/leaves/guides/overlapping-dates.png)

Open **History**, choose **All Types**, and find the existing report before submitting again. If the existing report needs a correction, use the guidance below. A **Denied** request does not block a new report for the same dates.

## Can I edit, cancel, or withdraw a report after submitting it?

Leaves currently has no edit, cancel, or withdraw action for submitted reports. **Request Detail** lets you review a report's dates, hours, status, and submission note.

If a submitted report needs a correction, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves). Identify the report by its leave type and dates, and describe the correction needed.

## Why was my request approved immediately?

Your department's approval setting determines what happens after submission. On the faculty dashboard, **Quick Actions** shows the **Approval type**.

- **Auto-approve** means the report is recorded as **Approved** when you submit it.
- **Approval required** means the report stays **PendingApproval** until a reviewer acts on it.

See [Finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status) for the status labels and where to check them.

## Should I receive an email when I submit a report?

For a request that needs review, the submission email goes to the department's configured recipients. You receive a notification when the request is approved or denied. For an automatically approved report, Leaves sends you an approval notification after submission.

You can check the current status in **History** without waiting for an email. If notifications seem to be missing, [contact Leaves support](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves).

## What should I do if a request is denied?

Open the request in **History** to check its dates, hours, type, and status. Contact your department's reviewer if you need to discuss the decision. If you need help with the application, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves).

## Why can I not see a tab or a faculty member?

Your access determines which tabs and records are available. Faculty use **Dashboard** and **History**. Department approvers use **Team Calendar** and **Approvals** for their assigned departments. CAOs use **CAO Calendar** and **Approvals** for their assigned clusters.

If you expected access that is missing, [contact Leaves support](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves) and describe which tab or faculty record you expected to see.

## Why can I not submit a report?

Read the message shown in the form and check the required fields. Leaves requires a current faculty record with accrual information and a reporting department before you can submit.

If the message says your faculty record or reporting department is missing, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves). These records cannot be changed from the leave-report form.

## Why did my balance not change after approval?

The displayed balances come from UCPath. Submitting or approving a request does not recalculate those balances in Leaves. See [Understanding leave balances](/documentation/leaves/understanding-leave-balances) for help reading the hours and accrual caps.
