Department approvers and CAOs can open a faculty member's dashboard to review leave balances and recent requests. This view is read-only.

## Open the faculty dashboard

1. Select **Team Calendar** or **CAO Calendar**.
2. Find the faculty member in the calendar.
3. Select their name or a leave block in their row.

The dashboard shows the person's name and contact information, their leave balances, recent requests, and an individual calendar. Check the name at the top before reviewing the records.

[![Read-only faculty dashboard showing Alex Morgan's contact information, vacation and sick leave balances, and five recent requests.](/leaves/guides/faculty-dashboard.png "Review a faculty member's balances and recent leave")](/leaves/guides/faculty-dashboard.png)

## Review balances and recent requests

The balance panel shows the available leave categories and their calculated balances in hours. Where a category has an accrual cap, a bar shows how much of that cap has been reached. Categories without a cap display **No accrual cap**.

**Recent Requests** shows the five most recently submitted requests, including each request's leave type, dates, hours, and status. Select a request to open **Request Detail**.

[![Request Detail dialog showing the faculty member, department, leave dates, hours, submission date, note, and request status.](/leaves/guides/faculty-request-detail.png "Read the details of an individual leave request")](/leaves/guides/faculty-request-detail.png)

Check the status near the top of the dialog, then review the dates, hours, submission date, and note. Select **Close** to return to the dashboard.

## Use the individual calendar

Use **Prev** and **Next** to move between months, or **Today** to return to the current month. Select a leave entry to open its details.

The legend identifies leave types, and stripes mark pending requests. Denied requests do not appear on the calendar. University holidays are highlighted, with a note below the calendar showing the available holiday coverage.

This dashboard covers recent activity. Its calendar includes up to the 24 most recently submitted requests, so an empty month does not establish that the faculty member has never reported leave for those dates. Faculty can use their own **History** page to find older requests.

## Return to your work

Select **Team Calendar** or **CAO Calendar** to return to the overview, or **Approvals** to review a pending request. The faculty dashboard does not let you submit leave for the person or change their requests.
