Department approvers and CAOs use the same **Approvals** page. The department approver role is called **Chair** in Leaves. Chairs review requests for their assigned departments; CAOs review requests for their assigned clusters.

## Open your pending requests

Select **Approvals** in the navigation bar. The number beside the tab shows how many requests are waiting for review.

Each request shows the faculty member's name, leave type, dates, total hours, and a note when one was provided. Read these details before making a decision.

[![Pending Approvals showing faculty leave requests with dates, hours, notes, and Approve and Deny buttons.](/leaves/guides/pending-approvals.png "Review the details of a pending leave request")](/leaves/guides/pending-approvals.png)

For more context, use **Team Calendar** or **CAO Calendar** to [open the faculty member's dashboard](/documentation/leaves/reviewing-faculty-leave). You can review their balances and recent requests there, then return to **Approvals**.

## Approve or deny a request

1. Find the request you want to review.
2. Check the faculty member, leave type, dates, hours, and note.
3. Select **Approve** or **Deny**.

**Your selection records the decision immediately.** There is no second confirmation or field for entering a reason. If you need to discuss the request with the faculty member, do that before choosing a decision.

A department approver or CAO can complete the review. Leaves does not require a second approval from the other role.

## Check the result

Leaves displays a confirmation and removes the request from the pending list. The faculty member receives an automated email about the decision and can check the status in their own request history.

[![Confirmation message stating that Alex Morgan's request was approved.](/leaves/guides/approval-confirmation.png "Confirmation after approving a leave request")](/leaves/guides/approval-confirmation.png)

Approved leave appears on the team or CAO calendar. Denied leave does not appear on the calendar. When the queue is empty, the page says **No pending approvals. All caught up!**

If Leaves says another approver already handled the request, the queue refreshes to reflect that decision. If it reports that the request could not be updated, try again.
