Professional Development records your dates for tracking. It is informational, so the form does not ask for hours or deduct hours from your leave balance.

## Enter the dates

1. On **Dashboard**, select **Report Leave** under **Quick Actions**.
2. Select **Professional Development** under **Type of Leave**. The form title changes to **Report Professional Development**.
3. Choose **Single Day** or **Date Range** and enter your dates.
4. Add a **Note** if you want to provide context, such as the event or activity.

You can also select dates on the dashboard calendar and then choose **Report Leave** to open the form with those dates filled in.

[![Report Professional Development form with the informational notice, date selection, optional note, and Submit Notification button.](/leaves/guides/professional-development-form.png "Enter professional development dates and an optional note")](/leaves/guides/professional-development-form.png)

## Submit the notification

Check the dates and select **Submit Notification**. Find the entry in **Recent Requests**, **History**, or your dashboard calendar.

Professional Development follows your department's Leaves approval setting. A department that requires review will show **PendingApproval** after submission; an auto-approval department will show **Approved**. The informational notice does not bypass that setting.

An existing pending or approved request for any leave type can block overlapping dates. Check your history if the form reports an overlap.

For help locating the notification or understanding its status, see [finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status). For corrections after submission, see [common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions).
