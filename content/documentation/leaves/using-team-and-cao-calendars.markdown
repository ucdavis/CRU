The calendar gives department approvers and CAOs a shared view of faculty leave. Each row represents a faculty member, and each column represents a day of the displayed month.

## Open your calendar

Select **Team Calendar** if you are a department approver, called **Chair** in Leaves. Your calendar includes faculty in your assigned departments.

[![Team Calendar with faculty names down the left and colored leave periods across the month.](/leaves/guides/team-calendar.png "Department leave at a glance")](/leaves/guides/team-calendar.png)

Select **CAO Calendar** if you are a CAO. The page is labeled **Cluster: Leave Overview** and includes faculty across your assigned clusters. The calendar controls work the same way.

[![CAO Calendar displaying the Cluster Leave Overview and faculty leave across the month.](/leaves/guides/cao-calendar.png "Review leave across your assigned clusters")](/leaves/guides/cao-calendar.png)

## Find a date and read the calendar

1. Check the month and year above the calendar.
2. Use **Prev** or **Next** to move between months.
3. Find the faculty member's row and follow it across to the dates you need. Scroll horizontally if the full month does not fit on your screen.

Use the legend below the calendar to identify each leave type. A striped leave block means the request is **Pending**. Solid leave blocks show approved leave. Denied requests do not appear here.

Weekend columns are shaded gray. University holidays are shaded blue; point to a holiday date to see its name. The note below the legend tells you how far the holiday information extends.

The overview shows the span of each leave period. To review the request's reported hours and note, open its details in the faculty dashboard.

## Open a faculty member's dashboard

Select a faculty member's name or one of their leave blocks. Their dashboard opens with balances, recent requests, and an individual calendar. Select a recent request or an entry on that calendar to view its details.

Use **Team Calendar** or **CAO Calendar** in the navigation bar to return to the overview. To make an approval decision, select **Approvals**.

If a faculty member changes departments or is no longer on the current roster, their pending request may still be in your approval queue. You can review that request from **Approvals** even if their dashboard is unavailable.
