For faculty checking their own balances and department approvers or CAOs reviewing a faculty member's record.

## Find your balances

Open **Dashboard** and look for **Leave Balances**. The panel identifies **UCPath** as its source and shows a separate balance for each available leave type.

[![Leave Balances panel showing UCPath as the source, hours by leave type, and accrual cap information.](/leaves/guides/leave-balances.png "Read your leave balances")](/leaves/guides/leave-balances.png)

Department approvers and CAOs can also view balances when they [open a faculty member's leave record](/documentation/leaves/reviewing-faculty-leave).

## Read the numbers

- An amount followed by **hrs** is the balance in hours for that leave type.
- A bar and **% of cap** show how close the balance is to the displayed accrual cap. The bar fills as the balance approaches the cap.
- **No accrual cap** means no cap is shown for that balance.

For example, the pictured vacation balance of 96 hours with a 240-hour cap is at 40% of its cap. That percentage describes the balance relative to the cap; it does not show how much leave you have taken.

Read each leave type separately. Vacation and sick leave have their own balances.

## Understand balances and submitted reports

Leaves displays balances supplied by UCPath. Submitting or approving a leave report does not recalculate those displayed balances in Leaves.

To confirm that a report was recorded or approved, use its status in **Recent Requests** or **History**. See [Finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status).

Professional development is informational and does not require hours. Sabbatical records the approved period, with vacation debits handled outside Leaves. See [Reporting professional development](/documentation/leaves/reporting-professional-development) or [Recording approved sabbatical or FMLA](/documentation/leaves/recording-approved-sabbatical-or-fmla) for those forms.

## Get help with a missing or unexpected balance

If Leaves displays **No current accrual balances are available**, it has no current balances to show for that faculty record. This message is different from a displayed balance of zero hours.

If a balance is missing or looks wrong, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves). Include the leave type and describe what you expected to see.
