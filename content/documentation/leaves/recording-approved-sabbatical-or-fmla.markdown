Sabbatical and FMLA must already be approved in [MyInfoVault](https://myinfovault.ucdavis.edu/) before you enter them in Leaves. Leaves records the approved period for calendar and tracking purposes.

Start either report by selecting **Report Leave** under **Quick Actions** on your **Dashboard**.

## Record a sabbatical

1. Select **Sabbatical** under **Type of Leave**. The form title changes to **Record Approved Leave**.
2. Read the notice and select **I confirm this sabbatical has been approved in MyInfoVault**.
3. Enter the approved **Start Date** and **End Date** under **Sabbatical Period**.
4. Add an optional **Note**.
5. Check your dates and select **Record Approved Leave**.

[![Sabbatical form with the MyInfoVault approval notice, confirmation checkbox, and approved period fields.](/leaves/guides/sabbatical-form.png "Record a sabbatical period approved in MyInfoVault")](/leaves/guides/sabbatical-form.png)

Sabbatical does not ask for leave hours. Vacation debits associated with a sabbatical are managed outside Leaves.

## Record FMLA leave

1. Select **FMLA** under **Type of Leave**.
2. Read the notice and select **I confirm this FMLA leave has been approved in MyInfoVault**.
3. If appropriate, choose a **Pay Type**. Available options can include **No pay / decide later**, **Vacation**, and **Sick**. Vacation and Sick show your available balance alongside the option.
4. Choose **Single Day** or **Date Range** and enter your approved leave dates.
5. For a range, review the weekend and university holiday exclusions used in the hours suggestion.
6. Enter **Total Hours** for the entire request and add an optional **Note**.
7. Check the form and select **Record Approved Leave**.

[![FMLA form with the MyInfoVault approval confirmation, optional pay type, dates, and Total Hours field.](/leaves/guides/fmla-form.png "Record approved FMLA dates, hours, and an optional pay type")](/leaves/guides/fmla-form.png)

Pay Type is optional. The Vacation and Sick choices appear when matching balance records are available. For more about the displayed hours, see [understanding leave balances](/documentation/leaves/understanding-leave-balances).

**Total Hours is the total for the whole request.** For example, enter `24` for three eight-hour days. FMLA requests require more than zero and no more than 240 total hours per request.

## Check the Leaves status

After submitting, open **History** or **Recent Requests** to check your entry. Prior approval in MyInfoVault and the request's status in Leaves are separate: your department's Leaves setting still determines whether the entry starts as **PendingApproval** or **Approved**.

See [finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status) for the history and calendar views. If the dates overlap an existing pending or approved request, or you need to correct a submitted entry, see [common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions).
