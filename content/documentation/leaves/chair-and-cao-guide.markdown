Chairs review leave for their assigned departments. CAOs review leave across their assigned clusters. Both roles use the same approval steps and calendar tools.

## Approve or deny a request

1. Select **Approvals** in the navigation bar. The number beside the tab shows how many requests need review.
2. Check the faculty member's name, leave type, dates, total hours, and note.
3. Select **Approve** or **Deny**.

<a href="/leaves/guides/pending-approvals.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="Open screenshot in a new tab"><img src="/leaves/guides/pending-approvals.png" alt="Pending Approvals with faculty names, leave dates, hours, notes, and Approve and Deny buttons." /></a>

**The decision takes effect immediately, with no second confirmation.** Discuss any questions with the faculty member before choosing a decision. Either an authorized Chair or CAO can complete the review; a second approval from the other role is not required.

Leaves confirms your decision, removes the request from the pending list, and emails the faculty member. Approved leave appears on the calendar.

## See who is away

Select **Team Calendar** as a Chair or **CAO Calendar** as a CAO. Each row is a faculty member and each column is a day. Use **Prev** and **Next** to change months.

<a href="/leaves/guides/cao-calendar.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="Open screenshot in a new tab"><img src="/leaves/guides/cao-calendar.png" alt="CAO calendar with faculty rows and colored leave periods across November." /></a>

The legend identifies leave types. Stripes mark pending requests; solid blocks show approved leave. Denied requests do not appear on the calendar.

## Check a faculty member's leave

Select a faculty member's name in the calendar to open their dashboard. Review their balances in hours and **Recent Requests**. Select a request to see its status, dates, hours, and note.

<a href="/leaves/guides/faculty-dashboard.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="Open screenshot in a new tab"><img src="/leaves/guides/faculty-dashboard.png" alt="Alex Morgan&#x27;s faculty dashboard showing vacation and sick leave balances alongside recent requests." /></a>

This is a read-only view of recent activity. Faculty can find their full history in their own **History** tab.
