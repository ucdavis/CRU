Use this guide to report **Vacation** or **Sick Leave**. For other leave types, see [reporting professional development](/documentation/leaves/reporting-professional-development) or [recording approved sabbatical or FMLA](/documentation/leaves/recording-approved-sabbatical-or-fmla).

## Start your report

Open **Dashboard** and select **Report Leave** under **Quick Actions**. You can also select **Report Leave** on the **History** page.

To start with dates already filled in, use the dashboard calendar:

1. Select a day for a single-day report, or select the first and last days of a date range.
2. Check the selection above the calendar. Select **Clear** if you need to start again.
3. Select **Report Leave** above the calendar.

[![Personal leave calendar with a date range selected and the Report Leave button available.](/leaves/guides/calendar-selected-range.png "Start a leave report from selected calendar dates")](/leaves/guides/calendar-selected-range.png)

## Enter your dates and total hours

1. Choose **Vacation** or **Sick Leave** under **Type of Leave**.
2. Choose **Single Day** or **Date Range**, then enter or check your dates.
3. For a range, review **Exclude weekends** and **Exclude university holidays**. These options change the suggested day count and hours shown below the dates.
4. Enter **Total Hours** for the entire request.
5. Add a **Note** if your reviewer needs more context.

**Total Hours means the total for the whole date range, not hours per day.** For example, three eight-hour days would be entered as `24`. The suggested hours use an eight-hour day; check that they match your actual leave and enter the total yourself.

[![Vacation report form with a date range, weekend and holiday exclusions, suggested hours, and a Total Hours field.](/leaves/guides/report-date-range.png "Review dates and enter the total hours for the request")](/leaves/guides/report-date-range.png)

Holiday suggestions cover only the dates currently available in Leaves. The form shows the coverage date, or a message if holiday data is unavailable. Review the dates and total hours before submitting.

## Submit and check the result

Select **Submit Leave Report**. Your report appears in **Recent Requests** on the dashboard and in **History**.

[![Recent Requests panel showing leave types, dates, hours, statuses, and Show in calendar actions.](/leaves/guides/recent-requests.png "Find your submitted report in Recent Requests")](/leaves/guides/recent-requests.png)

The **Approval type** shown under Quick Actions explains what happens next:

- **Approval required** sends the report for review. Its initial status is **PendingApproval**.
- **Auto-approve** records the report as **Approved** when you submit it.

Open the request to check its details. See [finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status) for history filters, status meanings, and calendar navigation.

## If the report will not submit

Vacation and sick leave require more than zero and no more than 240 total hours per request. An existing pending or approved request also blocks overlapping dates, even if you enter a different leave type or only part of a day.

Check **History** before trying again. For overlapping dates, incorrect submitted details, or missing profile information, see [common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions).
