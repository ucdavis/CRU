Use **Recent Requests** on your **Dashboard** for your five most recent reports. Open **History** for the full list, including denied requests.

## Find a request in History

1. Select **History** in the navigation.
2. Leave **All Types** selected to see every leave type, or choose a type to narrow the list.
3. Review the **Submitted**, **Leave Type**, **Date(s)**, **Hours**, and **Status** columns. Select a column heading to sort the table.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** below the table to move between pages.

[![Request History table with the All Types filter, submitted dates, leave types, dates, hours, statuses, and calendar actions.](/leaves/guides/request-history.png "Find and filter your leave history")](/leaves/guides/request-history.png)

**Submitted** is the date you sent the report. **Date(s)** is the period of leave, so use that column when looking for a particular absence.

## Open the details

Select a row or its submitted date to open **Request Detail**. You can also open a request from Recent Requests or by selecting its entry on the dashboard calendar.

The detail window shows the leave type and status, faculty member, department, dates, total hours, submission date, and your note. Select **Close** to return to the list.

[![Request Detail window showing the leave type, status, faculty member, department, dates, hours, submission date, and note.](/leaves/guides/request-detail.png "Review the details of a submitted request")](/leaves/guides/request-detail.png)

## Understand the status

- **PendingApproval:** Your department requires review and a reviewer has not yet recorded a decision.
- **Approved:** The request was approved by a reviewer or automatically when submitted, depending on your department's setting.
- **Denied:** A reviewer did not approve the request. It remains in History.

Your dashboard shows the department's **Approval type** under Quick Actions. Faculty receive an email when a request is approved automatically or when a reviewer approves or denies it. For a pending submission, check the status in Leaves; a separate faculty confirmation email is not sent at that stage.

## Find the leave on your calendar

Select **Show in calendar** beside a request in History or Recent Requests. Leaves opens the dashboard calendar at the request's start date.

Use **Prev**, **Next**, and **Today** to navigate. The legend identifies leave types, weekends, university holidays or breaks, and pending requests. Pending entries have a striped appearance. Select an entry to open its details.

[![Personal leave calendar with colored leave entries, pending styling, month controls, and a legend.](/leaves/guides/personal-calendar.png "Locate leave dates on your dashboard calendar")](/leaves/guides/personal-calendar.png)

Denied requests do not appear on the calendar. Use **History** to find them, even if **Show in calendar** takes you to the correct month.

Request Detail is a viewing window; it does not offer editing or cancellation. If you spot a mistake, see [common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions) before submitting another report for the same dates.
