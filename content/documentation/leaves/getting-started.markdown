Leaves helps faculty report leave and helps department approvers and CAOs review requests and see leave across their teams. Start at [leaves.ucdavis.edu](https://leaves.ucdavis.edu) and sign in.

The tabs you see depend on your access. Faculty use **Dashboard** and **History** for their own leave. Department approvers use **Team Calendar** and **Approvals**. CAOs use **CAO Calendar** and **Approvals**, with access across their assigned clusters. The application calls the department approver role **Chair**.

Screenshots use fictional example records. Select a screenshot to open it at full size.

## Report and review your own leave

Your dashboard brings together **Quick Actions**, **Recent Requests**, **Leave Balances**, and your calendar. Use **Report Leave** to start a report, or select dates on the calendar first.

[![Faculty dashboard with Report Leave, Recent Requests, and UCPath leave balances.](/leaves/guides/faculty-overview.png "Your faculty dashboard")](/leaves/guides/faculty-overview.png)

Choose the guide that matches what you need to do:

- [Submitting a leave request](/documentation/leaves/submitting-a-leave-request-v2): report vacation or sick leave.
- [Reporting professional development](/documentation/leaves/reporting-professional-development): record professional development dates.
- [Recording approved sabbatical or FMLA](/documentation/leaves/recording-approved-sabbatical-or-fmla): record leave already approved in MyInfoVault.
- [Finding requests and checking status](/documentation/leaves/finding-requests-and-checking-status): find an earlier report or check whether it was approved.
- [Understanding leave balances](/documentation/leaves/understanding-leave-balances): understand your hours and accrual caps.

## Review leave for your department or cluster

Department approvers and CAOs follow the same review steps. Your assignments determine which faculty and requests you can see.

[![Leaves navigation for a department approver, showing Team Calendar and Approvals.](/leaves/guides/approver-navigation.png "Navigation for department approvers")](/leaves/guides/approver-navigation.png)

- [Reviewing leave requests](/documentation/leaves/reviewing-leave-requests): approve or deny a pending request.
- [Using the team and CAO calendars](/documentation/leaves/using-team-and-cao-calendars): see who is on leave and navigate between months.
- [Reviewing a faculty member's leave](/documentation/leaves/reviewing-faculty-leave): look at their balances and leave record.

## Get help with a question or problem

[Common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions) covers overlapping dates, submitted reports that need a correction, missing access, and notification emails.

If you still need help, [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves).
