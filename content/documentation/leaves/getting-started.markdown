Start at [leaves.ucdavis.edu](https://leaves.ucdavis.edu) and sign in. The tabs you see depend on your role.

## Report your own leave

Your **Dashboard** brings together **Report Leave**, recent requests, balances, and your calendar. Open **History** to find earlier reports or check their status.

<a href="/leaves/guides/faculty-overview.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="Open screenshot in a new tab"><img src="/leaves/guides/faculty-overview.png" alt="Faculty dashboard with Report Leave, Recent Requests, and UCPath leave balances." /></a>

The [faculty guide](/documentation/leaves/faculty-guide-v2) walks through reporting leave and checking your requests.

## Review your team's leave

Department approvers and CAOs use **Approvals** to review requests and **Team Calendar** or **CAO Calendar** to see who is on leave. Leaves calls the department approver role **Chair**.

The [Chair and CAO guide](/documentation/leaves/chair-and-cao-guide) covers approvals, calendars, and viewing a faculty member's leave.

## Get help

Read the [FAQ](/documentation/leaves/common-questions) for corrections, overlapping dates, balances, and access problems, or [create a Leaves support ticket](https://caeshelp.ucdavis.edu/?appname=Leaves).
