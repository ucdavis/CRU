Use your **Dashboard** to report leave and see your calendar, recent requests, and balances from UCPath.

## Report vacation or sick leave

1. Select **Report Leave** under **Quick Actions**. You can also select a day or date range on the calendar, then select **Report Leave** above it.
2. Choose **Vacation** or **Sick Leave** under **Type of Leave**.
3. Choose **Single Day** or **Date Range** and enter your dates. For a range, review **Exclude weekends** and **Exclude university holidays** to adjust the suggested hours.
4. Enter **Total Hours** for the entire request. Three eight-hour days means `24`, not `8`. Check the suggestion and enter the total yourself.
5. Add an optional **Note**, check the form, and select **Submit Leave Report**.

## Report other leave types

Use the same **Type of Leave** menu:

- **Professional Development** records dates without leave hours. Add an optional note and select **Submit Notification**.
- **Sabbatical** records a period already approved in [MyInfoVault](https://myinfovault.ucdavis.edu/). Confirm that approval, enter the start and end dates, and select **Record Approved Leave**. No hours are required.
- **FMLA** also requires prior MyInfoVault approval. Confirm it, enter dates and total hours, optionally choose a **Pay Type**, and select **Record Approved Leave**.

All leave types follow your department's Leaves approval setting, including leave already approved in MyInfoVault.

## Check your requests

Open **History** to find reports and filter by leave type. Select a row for details, or choose **Show in calendar** to see the dates on your dashboard.

<a href="/leaves/guides/request-history.png" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="Open screenshot in a new tab"><img src="/leaves/guides/request-history.png" alt="Request History showing leave dates, hours, statuses, and Show in calendar actions." /></a>

**PendingApproval** means the report awaits review. **Approved** means it was approved automatically or by a reviewer. **Denied** reports remain in History but do not appear on the calendar.

For corrections, overlapping dates, or missing information, see [common questions](/documentation/leaves/common-questions).
